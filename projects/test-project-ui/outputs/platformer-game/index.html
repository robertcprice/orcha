GRAVITY FLUX

Manipulate gravity to navigate impossible platforms

How to Play

Arrow Keys / WASD

Move left and right

Space

Jump (press twice for double jump)

Left Click

Create gravity well at cursor (pulls you toward it)

Right Click

Create repulsion well at cursor (pushes you away)

R

Restart level

Tips:

  • Gravity wells have a 2-second cooldown
  • You can have up to 2 wells active at once
  • Chain wells together for complex maneuvers
  • Collect all stars for bonus points!
